Limitations & how to read this
  • No common definition of "AI revenue". TCS reports "AI services", Infosys "AI-first" revenue share, HCLTech "advanced AI" (incl. related data work), Accenture "advanced AI" (excl. data and classical AI). Levels are not strictly comparable; that is why every figure carries a grade and a definition.
  • Relabelling risk. Part of reported AI growth may be existing work reclassified as AI. The "core shrinking" result (H2) is therefore an upper bound on true cannibalisation.
  • Estimates for non-disclosers. Cognizant, Wipro and Tech Mahindra disclose no AI revenue; Capgemini gives only a share of bookings; Accenture's last quarterly AI revenue found is Q1 FY26. Their figures are ranges, shown as such.
  • Run-rate basis. Latest quarter × 4 ignores seasonality. Accenture's quarter is Jun–Aug, others Jul–Sep.
  • Currency and M&A. Capgemini converted at one EUR/USD rate for both quarters; its growth and headcount include WNS. HCLTech's latest USD revenue is derived from its reported growth rate.
  • Small sample. Rank correlations use 8 firms (4 for disclosers); treat them as directional, not statistical proof.
  • Scenarios are not forecasts. Levers are calibrated on observed 2025–26 AI and core growth and on published deflation estimates (Kotak 3–3.5%/yr; HCLTech 2–3%).
